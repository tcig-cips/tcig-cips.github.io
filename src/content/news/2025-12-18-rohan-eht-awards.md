[Rohan Dahale](/people/#rohan) received two awards from the [Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration](https://eventhorizontelescope.org/awards-eht): the **Outstanding PhD Thesis Award** — for showing that M87's ring ellipticity is governed by turbulent plasma dynamics and for establishing the validation framework that underpins the first robust videos of SgrA* — and the **EHT Early-Career Award 2025** — for his work on investigating the origin of the ring ellipticity in the black hole images of M87*. Congratulations, Rohan!
